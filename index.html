1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to Balasore 360 ("we", "our", or "the app"), developed by Nihar Bhuyan (niharbhuyan@gmail.com). This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection, usage, and user protection in accordance with Google Play Developer Program policies and applicable privacy regulations.

2. Information We Collect & Why

Balasore 360 does not collect, harvest, store, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII) such as names, passwords, phone numbers, or national identification numbers.

  • Local Preferences & Cache: Bookmarks, selected language (English/Odia), and offline viewing caches are stored locally on your device using Android Room Database. This data never leaves your device.
  • Diagnostics & Crash Data: Standard anonymous operating system diagnostic logs may be generated by the Android runtime to monitor stability and prevent application crashes.

3. Device Permissions & Purpose

We declare and request only the minimum necessary permissions strictly required to provide core hyper-local utility functions:

  • INTERNET: Required to retrieve updated tide tables, weather telemetries, river water levels, and local district news feeds.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to check internet connectivity and smoothly toggle offline caching modes.
  • CALL_PHONE: Used strictly for user-initiated 1-tap dialing to emergency services (e.g., Balasore Police 112, DHH Ambulance, Fire Control, and Cyclone Shelter in-charges). The app never initiates calls automatically without direct user interaction.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions & Troubleshooting

Quick troubleshooting solutions for permissions, location accuracy, offline data, and connectivity.

🛠️ 24x7 In-App Helpdesk
Permissions & GPS
Why is my location not displaying or showing "Location Permission Required"?

Balasore 360 uses your device GPS strictly on-device to compute real-time distances to the nearest cyclone shelters, hospitals, toto stands, and bus stops:

  • Enable App Permission: Open Android Settings → Apps → Balasore 360 → Permissions → Location, and select "Allow only while using the app".
  • Enable "Use Precise Location": For accurate walking distances at Chandipur Beach or Kasafal, ensure high accuracy/precise location is toggled on.
  • Coastal Satellite Lock: If you are inside thick concrete buildings or near the coast during overcast weather, step into open view of the sky for 5–10 seconds to acquire a stable satellite fix.
Connectivity
Why are river water levels, train arrivals, or bus schedules not refreshing?

If you experience intermittent 4G/5G mobile coverage in rural blocks of Balasore:

  • Check Data Connection: Verify that mobile data or Wi-Fi is active.
  • Battery Saver / Data Saver Mode: Android's battery saver may pause background network synchronizations. You can exempt Balasore 360 from battery optimization in your device settings.
  • Instant Manual Refresh: Open the Auto-Update & Live Telemetry Center from the City Essentials tab and tap "Force Sync All Data". This triggers an immediate fresh network pull for river gauges, train radar, and fish catch rates.
Offline Access
Can I use Balasore 360 during cyclones or when mobile data is completely down?

Yes, 100%! Balasore 360 is built with an offline-first architecture powered by an embedded local SQLite/Room database:

  • Zero Data Required: Emergency casualty numbers, 60+ multi-hazard cyclone shelter coordinates, police stations, toto fare charts, train timetables, and offline travel journals are saved locally on your phone.
  • Cached Telemetry: The last synchronized tidal windows, river gauge readings, and hospital ICU bed stock remain viewable even in flight mode.
Auto-Updates
How does background auto-refresh work and will it drain my phone's battery?

The app incorporates an intelligent Auto-Update Telemetry Engine managed by Android WorkManager:

  • Low Energy Overhead: Network requests are batched and executed in bursts every 15, 30, or 60 minutes when the device is unconstrained, consuming less than 0.5% battery per day.
  • Customizable Interval: You can choose your preferred auto-sync frequency (15m, 30m, 60m) or switch it to manual-only inside the Auto-Update Center at any time.
Language
How do I switch the app language between Odia (ଓଡ଼ିଆ) and English?

You can toggle the language instantly on any screen without restarting the app:

Simply tap the "ଓଡ଼ିଆ / English" pill in the top action bar. The entire interface, news feeds, proverbs, and feature descriptions will adapt dynamically to your selected language.
