Balasore360 Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 16, 2026

1. Introduction

Balasore360 ("the app") is an Android application published by the developer of the Balasore360 project. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the current version of the app.

2. Information We Collect

The current version of Balasore360 does not require an account, does not ask you to provide personal information, and does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or payment information.

The Android application declares Internet access so that future or network-enabled app functionality can communicate with online services. Internet access by itself does not mean that personal information is collected.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

The current version does not intentionally store personal information in a user account or personal profile. Android may create normal application/system files required for the app to operate. These are controlled by the Android operating system and are not intentionally used by the developer to identify you.

4. Analytics, Advertising and Third-Party Services

The current version of Balasore360 does not intentionally include advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or social-login services. If third-party services are added in a future version, this policy will be updated before or when those services are introduced, and the app's Google Play Data Safety information will be kept consistent with the actual data practices.

5. How Information Is Used

Because the current version does not intentionally collect personal information, there is no personal profile or personal-information database maintained by the developer for ordinary app use. Any technical information processed by Android or by a network service is handled according to the applicable platform or service terms and privacy policies.

6. Data Sharing

The developer does not intentionally sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties through the current version of the app. If a future feature requires data sharing, the relevant disclosures and this policy will be updated as required.

7. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to keep the application and its source code maintained. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

8. Children's Privacy

The current version of Balasore360 does not intentionally collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact the developer through the project repository so the matter can be reviewed.

9. Your Choices and Data Deletion

Because the current version does not maintain user accounts or an intentional personal-information database, there is no account data that a user needs to delete from the developer's systems. You can uninstall the app at any time using Android's normal uninstall controls.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when a new version is published.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, requests, or concerns regarding Balasore360, please contact the developer through the public Balasore360 GitHub repository issue page. Please do not post sensitive personal information publicly in an issue.

12. App Identification

App: Balasore 360
Android package: com.balasore360
