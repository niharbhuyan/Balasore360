1. INTRODUCTION
Nihar Sales ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Balasore 360 mobile application. This Privacy Policy informs users of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal and non-personal data when using Balasore 360, as well as the choices associated with that data in compliance with Google Play Developer Program Policies.

2. INFORMATION COLLECTION AND USE
We believe in minimal data collection and user privacy by design.

a. Offline-First Local Storage (Room Database)
- Tourist spot bookmarks, custom notes, search history, review drafts, and cached weather forecasts are stored locally on your device using an encrypted local Room SQLite database.
- You can clear your locally cached data at any time via the app settings or by clearing application data in Android System Settings.

b. User Account & Profile (Optional)
- If you voluntarily create an account or sign in to submit community reviews or report civic updates, we collect basic profile details: your name/alias, email address, and optional avatar URL.
- Authentication is handled securely, and passwords (if applicable) are never stored in plain text.

c. Community Reviews & Contributions (User-Generated Content)
- If you write a review, rate a tourist spot, or upload a photo of a Balasore attraction, this content is published publicly under your chosen display name.
- We do not access your full photo library; media selection utilizes the zero-permission Android Photo Picker (PickVisualMedia contract).

d. Location Data (Foreground Only, Optional)
- Balasore 360 may request foreground coarse or fine location permission to sort nearby tourist attractions (such as Chandipur Beach, Remuna, or Talasari) and provide localized coastal weather or tide timings.
- We DO NOT track your background location.
- We DO NOT sell, trade, or share your GPS coordinates with third parties.

e. Device & Diagnostics Information
- We may automatically collect non-identifiable technical data including device model, operating system version, and anonymous crash diagnostics to ensure app stability and performance.

3. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES
Balasore 360 integrates select third-party services that may process data according to their respective privacy policies:
- Google Play Services (Core Android runtime and updates)
- Firebase Cloud Messaging / FCM (Push notifications for critical weather alerts, tide updates, and breaking civic news - opt-out available in notification settings)
- Open-Meteo / Weather APIs (Fetching real-time atmospheric conditions and coastal tide data)

These third-party providers only have access to information necessary to perform their specific tasks and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose.

4. DATA SECURITY
The security of your data is important to us. We implement standard industry security practices, HTTPS/TLS encryption for network communications, and secure local device sandboxing to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

5. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION
- Locally cached data can be permanently erased by uninstalling the application or selecting "Clear Data" within your Android App Info settings.
- If you have an active account or submitted reviews and wish to request account deletion or removal of your user-generated content, you may submit a request directly to niharbhuyan@gmail.com. Requests are processed within 14 business days.

6. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
Balasore 360 is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. If you believe that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly delete such information.

7. CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY
We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be posted in this file and in the application store listing with an updated "Last Updated" date.
